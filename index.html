MAPPING THE ENTIRE SKY
MEET THE MISSION

Same universe.
Different light.

NASA’s SPHEREx space telescope surveys the entire sky in 102 colours of near-infrared light. Those wavelengths help scientists study everything from nearby stars to the history of galaxies.

Explore the official SPHEREx archive ↗
THE BIG IDEA

The sky is always
telling a story.

As SPHEREx builds repeated maps of the sky, scientists can study objects and cosmic environments in new ways. COSMOS is being designed to make those observations easier for everyone to explore.
